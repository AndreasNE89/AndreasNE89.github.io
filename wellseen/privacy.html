Wellseen — Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

AboutSupportPrivacy Policy

This policy covers Wellseen on iPhone and iPad (from the App Store) and on Android phones and tablets (from Google Play). Where the two apps differ, it says so.

Wellseen does not collect any data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no server. Your inspections, photos, notes and templates are stored only on your device. They leave it only when you share a report or export a backup yourself, or when you move to a new phone with your device's own transfer tool (see Moving to a new Android phone).

Who is responsible

Wellseen is made by Andreas Nelvik Engebretsen, an independent developer in Norway (“I”, “me”). For the personal data I do receive, which is only what you send me yourself (for example a support email), I am the data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) as it applies in Norway through the EEA Agreement and the Norwegian Personal Data Act.

What the app stores on your device

Wellseen stores what you enter or attach, in the app's private storage on your iPhone, iPad or Android device:

  • Inspections, checklist items and their Pass, Fail or N/A status.
  • Values you enter for items, such as meter readings and the number of keys.
  • Notes, photo captions and written report summaries.
  • Photos you take or add, the time each photo was taken, and marked-up copies of them. Photos are marked up only on iPhone and iPad; the Android app keeps a marked-up photo from a backup as it is.
  • For a finished inspection: the names, roles and drawn signatures of the people who signed, when each of them signed, and the signed PDF reports.
  • Your custom checklist templates.
  • Your details for reports: on iPhone and iPad your name, contact details, an optional business name, logo and signature (Settings › Your Details); on Android the name, contact details, business name and logo you give each inspection.
  • App settings: on iPhone and iPad for example paper size and the last known status of Wellseen Pro, so Pro features work at once when the app opens; on Android whether you have seen the introduction and the role you signed with last.

None of this is sent to me or to anyone else by the app.

Data collection

Wellseen does not collect data. It includes no analytics or advertising SDKs, does not track you across apps or websites, and makes no network requests of its own. Its App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”, and its Google Play Data safety section says that no data is collected or shared. The Android app does not have permission to use the internet, and it does not use the Android advertising ID.

The App Store and Google Play install and update the app. Wellseen sends them nothing you enter in the app.

iPhone and iPad: If you have chosen in the Settings app to share analytics with Apple and with app developers, Apple may pass crash reports and usage statistics about Wellseen on to me. They do not identify you, and you can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Android: If you have allowed your Android device to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google Play may show me statistics about how stable Wellseen is, such as crashes and times the app stopped responding (Google calls this Android vitals). Google shows them only when there is enough data to keep the reports anonymous, and they do not identify you. You can turn this off in your device's Google settings (on many devices, Settings › Google › Usage & diagnostics).

Sharing and export

Data leaves your device only when you choose to share or export it: for example when you share a PDF report or export a .wellseen backup file with the share sheet. These files can contain your inspections, photos, notes, captions, values, branding and signatures. Once you give a file to another app or person, they handle it under their own terms.

A .wellseen backup made on one device opens in Wellseen on iPhone, iPad and Android. Wellseen clears away its own leftover copies of backups it exported earlier; the file you saved or sent somewhere else stays there.

Backup files contain your photos and notes. Store and share them only where you trust.

Moving to a new Android phone

The Android app turns off Google's cloud backup, so its data is never copied to your Google account. When you set up a new phone with Android 12 or later and choose to copy apps and data straight from your old phone (by cable or Wi-Fi), your inspections, photos, signatures and settings may move with Wellseen. They go from your old phone to your new one, not to me. From Android 11 and earlier nothing is copied this way: export a .wellseen backup to keep a copy or to move your inspections.

Camera and photos

iPhone and iPad: Wellseen asks for camera access so you can take inspection photos, and for photo library access so you can add existing photos. It asks only when you start one of those actions, and the photos stay in the app.

Android: Wellseen asks for no permissions. Camera opens your phone's own camera app, which hands the photo you take to Wellseen. Library opens the Android photo picker (or the system file picker on older devices), and Wellseen receives only the photos you choose. It cannot see your other photos. The photos are saved in the app.

Microphone, dictation and writing help (iPhone and iPad)

On supported devices you can dictate notes, polish a note, and draft a report summary. All of this runs on your device, using the speech recognition and language models built into iOS. Audio is not recorded or stored, and neither your speech nor your text is sent to any server. The first time you dictate, iOS may download its speech model for your language. Wellseen asks for microphone access only when you tap Dictate.

The Android app has no dictation or writing help and never uses the microphone.

Purchases (iPhone and iPad)

Wellseen Pro is sold through Apple's in-app purchase system. Payment is handled by Apple under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. The app never sees your payment details or your Apple Account. It receives only the status of your purchase (which Pro plan is active and until when) from Apple on your device, and uses it to unlock Pro features. Nothing about your purchase is stored anywhere else or sent to me. Restore Purchases asks Apple for purchases already made with your Apple Account. Pro subscriptions follow Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement.

The Android app is free and has no purchases.

Keeping your data safe

Wellseen keeps its data in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read, and your device's own lock and encryption protect it. A signed report is stored as it was signed and is not changed afterwards.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete inspections, photos and templates in the app. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on that device; on Android, so does clearing the app's storage in Settings. Files you have shared or exported are not removed: delete them where you keep them.

If you email me

If you write to me for support, I receive your email address and what you send, and use it only to answer you. I keep the conversation in my email account for as long as it is useful for helping you, and delete it on request.

Your rights

Because the app collects nothing, I hold no data about you unless you email me. For anything you have sent me, you can ask for a copy, a correction or deletion by writing to the address below. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet.

Children

Wellseen is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
