Wellseen — Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 September 2026

AboutSupportPrivacy Policy

Wellseen does not collect any data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no server. Your inspections, photos, notes and templates are stored only on your device, and they leave it only when you share a report or export a backup yourself.

Who is responsible

Wellseen is made by Andreas Nelvik Engebretsen, an independent developer in Norway (“I”, “me”). For the personal data I do receive, which is only what you send me yourself (for example a support email), I am the data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) as it applies in Norway through the EEA Agreement and the Norwegian Personal Data Act.

What the app stores on your device

Wellseen stores what you enter or attach, in the app's private storage on your iPhone or iPad:

  • Inspections, checklist items and their Pass, Fail or N/A status.
  • Notes, photo captions and written report summaries.
  • Photos you take or add, and marked-up copies of them.
  • Your custom checklist templates.
  • Your details for reports (your name, contact details, an optional business name, logo and signature).
  • App settings, such as paper size, and the last known status of Wellseen Pro so Pro features work at once when the app opens.

None of this is sent to me or to anyone else by the app.

Data collection

Wellseen does not collect data. It includes no analytics or advertising SDKs, does not track you across apps or websites, and makes no network requests of its own. Its App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”.

If you have chosen in the Settings app to share analytics with Apple and with app developers, Apple may pass crash reports and usage statistics about Wellseen on to me. They do not identify you, and you can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Sharing and export

Data leaves your device only when you choose to share or export it: for example when you share a PDF report or export a .wellseen backup file with the share sheet. These files can contain your inspections, photos, notes, captions, branding and signatures. Once you give a file to another app or person, they handle it under their own terms.

Backup files contain your photos and notes. Store and share them only where you trust.

Camera and photos

Wellseen asks for camera access so you can take inspection photos, and for photo library access so you can add existing photos. It asks only when you start one of those actions, and the photos stay in the app.

Microphone, dictation and writing help

On supported devices you can dictate notes, polish a note, and draft a report summary. All of this runs on your device, using the speech recognition and language models built into iOS. Audio is not recorded or stored, and neither your speech nor your text is sent to any server. The first time you dictate, iOS may download its speech model for your language. Wellseen asks for microphone access only when you tap Dictate.

Purchases

Wellseen Pro is sold through Apple's in-app purchase system. Payment is handled by Apple under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. The app never sees your payment details or your Apple Account. It receives only the status of your purchase (which Pro plan is active and until when) from Apple on your device, and uses it to unlock Pro features. Nothing about your purchase is stored anywhere else or sent to me. Restore Purchases asks Apple for purchases already made with your Apple Account. Pro subscriptions follow Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement.

If you email me

If you write to me for support, I receive your email address and what you send, and use it only to answer you. I keep the conversation in my email account for as long as it is useful for helping you, and delete it on request.

Your rights

Because the app collects nothing, I hold no data about you unless you email me. For anything you have sent me, you can ask for a copy, a correction or deletion by writing to the address below. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet.

Children

Wellseen is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date at the top.
