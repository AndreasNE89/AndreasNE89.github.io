Your inspections and backups

Do I need an account?

No. Wellseen has no accounts or sign-in. Your inspections, photos and templates are stored only on your device.

How do I back up, or move to a new iPhone?

Open Settings › Backup & Restore and export all data. This saves a .wellseen backup file that you can keep in Files, iCloud Drive or anywhere you like. On the new device, open that file (or use Import in the same screen). Keep backup files private: they contain your photos and notes.

A backup carries your inspections, photos, each inspection's own branding and signature, report summaries and custom templates. Your details in Settings (name, business name, logo, signature) are set up again on a new device.

How do I delete my data?

Delete inspections in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. There is nothing stored anywhere else.

Wellseen Pro

What does Pro include?

  • Your logo on reports, and no “Made with Wellseen” footer.
  • Writing help on the device: polish a note into clear sentences, and draft a report summary.
  • Re-inspection of failed items.
  • Unlimited custom templates (the free version keeps two).

Everything else, including unlimited inspections and PDF reports, is free.

I bought Pro. How do I get it on another device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Settings › Restore Purchases in Wellseen.

How do I cancel a subscription?

In Wellseen, tap Settings › Manage Subscription, or open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next charge. You keep Pro until the period ends.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Features

Why don't I see Polish or Write with AI?

Writing help uses Apple Intelligence, which runs on the device. It appears on iPhones and iPads that support Apple Intelligence, with it turned on in the Settings app. You can always write or use the plain summary yourself.

Dictation doesn't start the first time.

The first time you dictate, iOS may download its speech model for your language. Keep the app open with a network connection for a minute, then try again.

Which languages and devices are supported?

English and Norwegian, on iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or later.
