Wellseen

Inspection reports for anyone

AboutSupportPrivacy Policy

Wellseen turns a walk through a place into a clear, dated PDF report with photos, so you have a record of what you found and when.

It is free to download and use, needs no account, and works offline. Everything stays on your iPhone or iPad until you share a report.

Who it is for

  • Renters: record every room at move-in and move-out, with photos, in case the deposit is questioned.
  • Home buyers: go through a new home at handover and list what needs fixing.
  • Homeowners and holiday-rental hosts: keep a dated record of damage, repairs and the state of the place between guests.
  • Independent tradespeople, landlords and maintenance technicians: punch lists, move-in checks and equipment checks with photo evidence.

How it works

  • Start from a built-in checklist or make your own.
  • Mark each item Pass, Fail or N/A, add photos and mark them up, and type or dictate notes.
  • Sign, and share a PDF with a cover page, a list of everything that needs attention, and your photos.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking, no server. Dictation and writing help run on the device with Apple's built-in models. Read the privacy policy.

Free, with optional Pro

Inspections, photos and PDF reports are free and unlimited. Wellseen Pro adds your logo on reports without the Wellseen footer, on-device writing help, re-inspection of failed items, and unlimited custom templates. Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time purchase, through your Apple Account.

Wellseen is available in English and Norwegian and needs iOS 26 or later. Questions? See Support.
