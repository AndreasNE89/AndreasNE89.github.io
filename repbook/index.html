Repbook Iron Plan

A simple, fast workout log for people who lift weights.

Log every set. See your strength grow.

  • Save routines with the sets, reps, weight and rest you use most, from a library of 135 exercises or your own.
  • Log a workout set by set, with next-set guidance from your recent sets and a rest timer after every set.
  • Follow your progress: workouts this week, personal records, weekly volume and the exercises improving most.
  • Kilograms or pounds, light or dark.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. Your training log stays on your devices.

iPhone, Apple Watch and Android

Repbook Iron Plan is on the App Store for iPhone, with an Apple Watch app for logging at the gym. On Android phones it is on Google Play, where the app is called Repbook on your home screen, in English and Norwegian.

A backup file moves your whole training log between them: export it on one phone and restore it on the other.

On Android, Repbook Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play that adds your estimated 1-rep max over time, your heaviest weight at each rep count and a spreadsheet export. Everything else works without it.
