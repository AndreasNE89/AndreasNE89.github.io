Your training log

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no sign-in. Your routines and workouts are stored on your phone (and on your Apple Watch, if you use it). Nothing is sent to me or to a server.

How do I back up my training log?

Go to Settings › Backup › Export backup and save the file somewhere you control: your phone’s storage, a computer, or a cloud drive such as iCloud Drive or Google Drive. Do this before you uninstall the app or change phones: uninstalling removes the training log from the phone.

How do I restore a backup?

Go to Settings › Backup › Restore backup and pick the file. The app shows how many routines, workouts and sets the backup holds before anything changes. Restoring merges the backup with what is already on the phone: it adds what is missing and never deletes anything.

Can I move between iPhone and Android?

Yes. A backup exported on iPhone restores on Android, and the other way round. Export it on the old phone, move the file to the new one (for example through a cloud drive or email to yourself), then restore it there.

I got a new Android phone. Do I have to do anything?

If the new phone has Android 12 or later and you copy apps and data straight from your old phone while setting it up, your training log usually moves with the app. Repbook is not part of Google’s cloud backup, so restoring a new phone from your Google account does not bring it back. To be safe, export a backup on the old phone first.

Repbook Pro (Android)

What does Pro include?

Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play, not a subscription. It adds how your estimated 1-rep max has moved over time for every exercise, your heaviest weight at 1, 3, 5, 8, 10 and 12 reps, and exporting every logged set to a spreadsheet (CSV). Everything else, including routines, logging, guidance, history, progress and backups, works without it.

I bought Pro but it is not unlocked

  1. Make sure the Play Store on your phone is signed in to the Google account you bought Pro with.
  2. Go to Settings › Repbook Pro › Restore purchase while you are online.
  3. If the purchase is waiting for approval (a slow payment method, or a family member who has to approve it), Pro unlocks as soon as Google Play confirms it.

Still locked? Email me with the order number from your Google Play receipt (it starts with GPA.).

Does Pro work on my other phone?

Yes, on any Android phone signed in to the same Google account: install Repbook and use Restore purchase. Pro bought on Google Play does not unlock anything on iPhone, which has no Pro.

How do I get a refund?

Within 48 hours of buying, you can ask Google Play for a refund yourself: open your Google Play order history and choose Request a refund, or see Google’s refund help. After that, email me with the order number and I will look at it.

Using the app

Kilograms or pounds?

Settings › Weight unit. The app starts in pounds in the United States and a few other countries, and in kilograms everywhere else. Switching changes how weights are shown, not your logged sets.

Opening the spreadsheet export in Excel

When the app is in Norwegian, the CSV file uses semicolons between columns and decimal commas, as Norwegian Excel expects. In English it uses commas and decimal points. Both are UTF-8, so æ, ø and å come out right.

Which languages are there?

The Android app is in English and Norwegian (Bokmål) and follows your phone’s language. On Android 13 and later you can choose a language for Repbook alone in your phone’s settings (System › Languages › App languages). The iPhone and Apple Watch app is in English.

How do I delete my data?

Delete workouts, sets and routines in the app, or uninstall it to remove everything it stored on the phone. Exported files stay where you saved them. The privacy policy has the details.
