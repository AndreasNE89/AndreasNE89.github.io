Kinglet — Privacy policy

Chat and code with AI models that run on your Mac

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Kinglet is a chat app for AI models that run on your Mac. It has no account, no server, no analytics and no ads. The developer collects no data about you or how you use Kinglet.

Your chats and files stay on your Mac

Chats, projects, skills and settings are saved in Kinglet's own folder on your Mac. The models answer on your Mac. Kinglet reads the project folders you add, and changes a file only after you allow the change. Files you attach to a chat are copied into Kinglet's own folder on your Mac (the text Kinglet read from them, and pictures) and deleted with the chat; attached folders are read, never changed.

Calendar and Reminders

If you allow it, Kinglet reads your calendar when you ask about your schedule, and adds the reminders you approve. This stays on your Mac.

Shortcuts

Kinglet runs only the shortcuts you list in Settings, and only after you allow it. What a shortcut does is up to the shortcut.

When Kinglet uses the internet

  • Finding and downloading a model: when you search in Settings > Models, Kinglet sends what you type to Hugging Face (huggingface.co) to find models, and downloads the model you pick from there. Hugging Face sees these requests, as any website does, under its own privacy policy. Kinglet sends it nothing else.
  • Connections: if you add a remote MCP server, the messages that mention it, and what the model sends to its tools, go to that server under its owner's privacy policy. Its token is kept in your Mac's Keychain.

Kinglet contacts no other server.

Deleting your data

Delete a chat (with what was attached to it), a project or a model (Settings > Models) in Kinglet. Moving the app to the Trash doesn't remove what it saved; to remove everything, also move the folder ~/Library/Containers/com.andreasengebretsen.wren to the Trash.

Children

Kinglet is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
